# Electric-Vehicles-Sales-Analysis
Analyzed India’s EV market trends using historical sales data to identify key growth drivers, top-performing manufacturers, and optimal states for setting up manufacturing units.

## 📌 About Company
**AtliQ Motors** is a leading automotive company based in the USA, specializing in Electric Vehicles (EVs) and Hybrid Vehicles. Over the last 5 years, the company has achieved a 25% market share in the EV/Hybrid segment in North America.
As part of its global expansion strategy, AtliQ Motors plans to enter the **Indian EV market**, where its current market share is less than **2%**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Before launching its bestselling EV models in India, the management team wanted to perform a detailed market study of the Indian EV industry.
The objective was to understand:

- Market demand and growth trends  
- Customer adoption patterns  
- Competitor performance  
- State-wise EV opportunities  
- Manufacturing feasibility  
- Strategic recommendations for successful expansion

## 📊 Main Measures Created

-Total Sales – Calculates total vehicles sold (EV + non-EV).   
-Total EV Sales – Calculates total electric vehicles sold.   
-Makers Revenue – Estimates revenue generated by each EV maker based on units sold and average price.   
-Projected EV Sales for 2030 – Forecasts future EV sales using historical CAGR growth.   
-CAGR % – Measures the average annual growth rate over a selected period.   
-Penetration Rate % – Shows EV sales as a percentage of total vehicle sales.   
-State EV Revenue – Estimates EV revenue generated in each state.   
-States Market Share – Shows each state's contribution to total EV sales.   
-YoY Growth % – Measures year-over-year growth in EV sales.   

## 💡 Key Insights

* The 2-Wheeler EV segment shows higher demand and revenue potential than 4-Wheelers.   
* EV sales peak during Q1 and Q4, indicating seasonal demand trends.   
* Ola Electric, TVS, Ather, and Ampere lead the 2W EV market.   
* Tata Motors and Mahindra dominate the 4W EV market.   
* Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Gujarat lead EV sales across states.   
* Chhattisgarh and Goa show strong growth potential with high CAGR.   
* Charging infrastructure has a strong positive impact on EV adoption.   
* Gujarat is the most suitable state for EV manufacturing setup.   

## 🚀 Final Recommendations

🔹 **1. Enter through the 2-Wheeler EV segment**   
Focus on the 2W segment for initial entry as it has higher demand, faster adoption, and lower market concentration compared to 4W, making it more suitable for new market entry.   
🔹 **2. Establish manufacturing in Gujarat**   
Set up the manufacturing unit in Gujarat due to strong subsidies, lower operational costs, and a business-friendly policy environment, making it the most cost-efficient location.   
🔹 **3. Expand first in high-demand states**   
Prioritize Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu for early expansion as these states lead in EV sales, penetration, and charging infrastructure availability.   
🔹**4. Target emerging high-growth markets**   
After establishing presence, expand into emerging states like Chhattisgarh and Goa, which show very high CAGR and strong future growth potential despite lower current penetration.   
🔹 **5. Align expansion with charging infrastructure and seasonality**   
Focus on regions with strong charging infrastructure, as it has a strong positive correlation with EV adoption. Also plan market pushes during Q1 and Q4 when EV sales peak due to festive demand and seasonal trends.
